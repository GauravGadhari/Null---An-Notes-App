Privacy Policy for Null

Effective Date: August 20, 2026 • Application: Null - Pure Dark Notes

✦ Summary of Our Commitment

Null is built with zero compromise on your privacy. We collect zero personal information, require no accounts, track no analytics, and store all notes strictly on your local device using high-speed binary storage.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Your thoughts, drafts, typography formatting, and settings belong only to you.

2. Local Device Storage

All notes and settings are persisted locally in an offline binary database (Hive) inside your device's sandboxed storage. Data never leaves your device and is permanently removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Zero Third-Party Tracking & No Ads

Null contains zero third-party tracking frameworks, zero advertising SDKs, and zero telemetry.

4. Children's Privacy

Because no personal data is ever collected, Null complies with COPPA and is safe for users of all ages.
