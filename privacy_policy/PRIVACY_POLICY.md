# Privacy Policy for Null

**Effective Date:** August 20, 2026  
**Application Name:** Null - Pure Dark Notes  
**Package Name:** `com.light.null_notes`  
**Developer:** Gaurav Gadhari  

---

## ✦ Overview & Commitment
Welcome to **Null** ("we," "our," or "us"). We believe your thoughts, ideas, and notes belong solely to you. Null is engineered as an **offline-first, zero-telemetry, privacy-centric** application. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict non-collection data practices.

---

## 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
**Null does not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal data on external servers.**
- **No User Accounts:** Null does not require registration, email sign-ups, phone numbers, or social logins.
- **No Note Syncing to External Clouds:** All written text, formatting spans, custom typography choices, and background color settings remain strictly within your device's sandboxed local storage.
- **No Analytics or Telemetry:** Null contains zero tracking libraries, user activity loggers, device fingerprinting tools, or behavioral analytics SDKs.

---

## 2. Local Device Storage (Hive Binary Database)
- All notes and user preferences (such as "Open to New Note" and "Smart Words Styling") are saved in a high-performance local binary database (**Hive**) on your device.
- This data is isolated in the application's private sandbox and is never transmitted over any network.
- **Data Deletion:** Uninstalling the application or clearing app data via your device settings immediately and permanently deletes all locally stored notes and preferences.

---

## 3. Network & Device Permissions
- **Zero Internet Requirement:** Null functions completely offline without an active internet connection.
- **No Sensitive Permissions:** Null does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, biometric sensors, or external media libraries.

---

## 4. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs
- Null is **100% ad-free**.
- Null contains **no third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or monetization frameworks**.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Because Null does not collect, process, or transmit any personal information whatsoever, it does not collect information from children under the age of 13. Null is safe and compliant for users of all ages.

---

## 6. International Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)
- **GDPR (European Union):** Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your data. Because all data is stored exclusively on your local device, you retain total ownership and control over your content at all times.
- **CCPA / CPRA (California, USA):** We do not sell or share personal information as defined by California privacy laws, because zero personal data is collected or transferred.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If we make any modifications to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
