Privacy Policy for Null

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Summary: Null is a 100% offline, privacy-first notes app. We do not collect, track, store on remote servers, or share any of your personal data or notes.

1. Zero Data Collection

Null does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All notes, styles, and settings remain strictly on your local device.

2. Permissions & Storage

Null operates offline and stores all content locally in the app's secure sandboxed storage.

3. Third-Party Services

Null does not include advertising SDKs, tracking tools, or third-party analytics.
